This is MVP version two. First, the home screen now explains the name: we hand the scam to the people who should stop it, not reply to the scammer. Second, a Known senders list. It is saved on the device in localStorage, and you can export it as JSON. Third, when a message comes from a saved sender, the app adds a REPEAT stamp, which adds 36 to the score, capped at 100. Fourth, a Write a follow-up button. It makes a complaint draft for Gmail, Outlook, a generic email, or the phone operator and WhatsApp, and it cites the first report date and the evidence. Finally, buttons open IASC, CekRekening and Patroli Siber. Nothing is sent automatically; the user submits it. Links: iasc.ojk.go.id, cekrekening.id, patrolisiber.id.

This is Layer 2, the network layer. On 16 November we ran a Containerlab lab on Fedora with Podman. Four nodes: a victim PC, a gateway firewall, a DNS filter, and a static fake JNE DEMO page — no forms, no credentials. The demo domain is jne-paket-tertunda.example. With the filter off, DNS returns 10.10.2.66 and the page opens with HTTP 200. With the filter on, DNS answers NXDOMAIN, curl by name cannot resolve the host, and curl to the IP times out after five seconds, because the gateway drops traffic to 10.10.2.66.

Law angle: whoever runs the system must secure it.

This is the law angle, which is our group's role. Our point is the provider's duty of care. Under UU ITE Articles 15 and 16, electronic system operators must run their systems reliably and securely, and they are responsible for them. Under UU PDP, Law 27 of 2022, Articles 35, 39 and 47, the data controller must secure personal data, prevent unauthorized access, and is accountable. So a documented repeat report changes the story from "we didn't know" to "you were told". We also have a policy idea: institutions can require Chrome Enhanced Safe Browsing through the policy SafeBrowsingProtectionLevel equals 2. Google says Enhanced Protection users are phished 20 to 35 percent less. But more browsing data goes to Google, so under UU PDP the institution needs a privacy notice.
